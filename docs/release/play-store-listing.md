## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Architect AR turns a Revit/BIM export into a shareable 3D model your
clients can explore from their phone — no app required to view it, no
CAD software to open it.

VIEW IN AR, AT REAL SCALE
Place the model in the room you're standing in, or on the actual site,
at the exact scale it was designed at. Walk around it. See it as it will
really look, before it's built.

TAP ANY ELEMENT FOR ITS DATA
Tap a wall, a door, a piece of equipment — see its real Revit data:
family and type, level, material, dimensions. The same information your
BIM model already carries, now reachable by anyone with the link.

SHARE WITH ONE LINK
Every project gets its own shareable, optionally passcode-protected
link. No account needed on the client's end — open it, look around,
done.

QUANTITY TAKEOFF
A category-organized takeoff (areas, lengths, counts) generated straight
from the model, exportable to Excel or CSV.

BUILT FOR ARCHITECTS
Multiple models per project, level and category filtering, lighting
presets, and a dashboard for managing every project and tracking who's
actually opened the link.

This app is built and used by Hiten Sethi & Associates for real client
work.
```

## Category

Business (alternates: Productivity, House & Home)

## Content rating

No user-generated content visible to other users, no ads, no in-app
purchases, no account creation by strangers (project access is
owner-managed). Should qualify for the lowest content-rating tier
("Everyone") — confirm by actually completing Play Console's rating
questionnaire, this is not a substitute for it.

## Target audience

Professional/business use (architecture firms and their clients), not
children. Set the target age group accordingly in Play Console.

## Permissions disclosure

The app requests **Camera** access, used only for AR (viewing the model
overlaid on the real world through the camera) — never for photos,
video recording, or any other purpose. State this plainly in Play
Console's permissions declaration; see
[`privacy-policy.md`](privacy-policy.md) for the fuller privacy text
this needs to match.
