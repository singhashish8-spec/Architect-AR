## What this app is

Architect AR is a tool built and used by Hiten Sethi & Associates to
share 3D architectural models with clients, for viewing on the web and
in augmented reality.

## Information we collect

**Project viewing.** When someone opens a shared project link, we record
that a view happened: which project, which model, when, and how long the
tab stayed open and visible. This is tied to the project being viewed,
not to any personal identity — we do not collect names, email addresses,
IP addresses, or device identifiers as part of this.

**Camera access (AR feature).** The AR walkthrough feature requests
access to your device's camera to overlay the 3D model on your real-world
surroundings. Camera video is processed on your device to detect
surfaces and track the phone's position — it is never recorded, stored,
or transmitted anywhere, by this app or to us.

**Uploaded project files.** Architects using the admin dashboard upload
3D models and BIM data files, which are stored on our infrastructure
(Cloudflare R2 object storage and a Supabase database) to make them
available at the shared link. These files are provided directly by our
architects for their own projects, not by app users browsing a shared
link.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, microphone audio,
photos or videos from your device's gallery, or any advertising
identifiers. We do not show ads and do not use third-party analytics or
advertising SDKs.

## How information is used

View data (see above) is used only to let the architects who own a
project see whether and how their shared links are being used — it is
never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond that.

## Data retention and deletion

Project and view data is retained for as long as the associated project
exists in the system. An architect can delete a project (and its
associated view history) at any time through the admin dashboard.

## Children's privacy

This app is a business tool for architecture firms and their clients and
is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information
from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continued use of the app
after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
